GSO — the shared layer

The GCC Standardization Organization (GSO) issues unified food standards that member states adopt or reference nationally — covering nutrition labeling, food-contact materials, additive limits, and category-specific rules (infant formula, marketing to children, shelf-life). National authorities below enforce these alongside their own import and registration regimes.

Country-by-country requirements

Each state runs its own import registration platform in addition to GSO standards. All six currently require halal certification and Arabic-language labeling for food sold in-market.

🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia

Authority
Saudi Food & Drug Authority (SFDA)
Platform
Ghad (importer & product registration) + SABER (conformity & shipment certificates)
Halal required Arabic label

2025: since 1 Oct 2025, customs will not clear goods without a SABER Shipment Certificate.

🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates

Authority
Ministry of Industry & Advanced Technology (MoIAT, formerly ESMA) + emirate municipalities
Platform
ZAD (federal registration) + emirate portals (e.g. Dubai Municipality FIRS, Abu Dhabi ZADI)
Halal required Bilingual label

Note: every product needs both federal ZAD registration and the relevant emirate's customs/municipality clearance — the two layers aren't interchangeable.

🇶🇦 Qatar

Authority
Ministry of Public Health (MOPH), Food Safety Department
Platform
Watheq electronic food-safety system
Halal required Arabic label

2025: adopted QS 10050:2025, a new technical regulation governing food shelf-life declarations.

🇰🇼 Kuwait

Authority
Public Authority for Food & Nutrition (PAFN); PAI for production facilities
Platform
PAFN product registration (per-product fee); KUCAS conformity scheme for general goods
Halal required Arabic label

Note: halal oversight runs through Kuwait's National Halal Food Committee, separate from PAFN's safety/nutrition review.

🇧🇭 Bahrain

Authority
Ministry of Health, Public Health Directorate (Food Control Section)
Platform
Pre-import approval via the OFOQ Bahrain customs single window
Halal required Arabic label

Note: "special food" categories — infant formula, supplements, energy drinks — need a separate dedicated pre-import license on top of standard clearance.

🇴🇲 Oman

Authority
Food Safety & Quality Center (FSQC), Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries & Water Resources
Platform
Mandatory pre-registration for all food, local or imported
Halal required Arabic label

2025: mandatory food pre-registration took effect 1 Jan 2025, building a national food-traceability database.

2025 regulatory timeline

The changes that moved this year across the bloc, in order.

  1. 1 Jan 2025 Oman — mandatory pre-registration of all food products (local and imported) takes effect under FSQC/MAFWR.
  2. 1 Oct 2025 Saudi Arabia — a SABER Shipment Certificate becomes a hard requirement for customs clearance of imported goods.
  3. Late 2025 Qatar — QS 10050:2025 shelf-life technical regulation adopted, setting new rules for shelf-life declarations.
  4. 2025 (consultation open to Sep 2026) GSO — draft GSO 2233:2025 nutrition labeling standard circulated for public comment, alongside new standards for infant formula (GSO 2106:2025) and marketing food to children (GSO 2725:2025).
